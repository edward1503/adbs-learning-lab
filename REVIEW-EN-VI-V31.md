## Why the original wording felt difficult

The inspected v3.0 often put a dense concept inventory before establishing what the reader was trying to explain. L2 `l2-1` introduced several SQL clauses before connecting row-level and group-level conditions. L7 introduced workload, layout, PAX, Parquet and compression together. L9’s opening named several repair operations while the worked insertion focused on leaves. That makes the reader supply the causal bridges even when individual definitions are correct.

The style reference [Agentic SWE chapter 1](https://book.agentic-swe.dev/chapter_1.html) was used for the general approach of motivating terms with a concrete consequence, explaining causes and carrying examples through the argument. No wording or unrelated SWE case was copied; it is not an ADBS factual source. The translated bodies use connected paragraphs and stepwise examples. Bullets or numbered traces organize genuine sequences instead of substituting for explanations.
